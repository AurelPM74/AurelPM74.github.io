abstract: This preprint studies quantum attacks on iterated two-key Even–Mansour ciphers, a model for block ciphers such as LED. It introduces attacks with better
  asymptotic complexity than exhaustive key search for four to six rounds, requiring only classical known-plaintext queries. For four rounds, the authors give two
  collision-search attacks and a quantum adaptation of a multibridge attack based on a quantum walk; the latter achieves time up to 2^(7n/9), compared with 2^n for
  exhaustive search, where n is the block size. A multicollision-based construction for six rounds yields a smaller asymptotic improvement. This is a paraphrased
  summary; see the linked ePrint for the official abstract.
